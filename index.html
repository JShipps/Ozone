III — The Chemistry

Four steps, running
a billion times a second

The Chapman cycle: sunlight builds ozone, sunlight unbuilds it, and the planet ends up wearing sunscreen.

1
O2 + UV → 2O
High-energy sunlight cracks ordinary oxygen apart.
2
O + O2 → O3
A free oxygen atom adopts a partner — ozone is born.
3
O3 + UV → O2 + O
Ozone soaks up deadly UV and quietly splits again.
4
O3 + O → 2O2
The cycle closes. Sunscreen for a planet, self-rolling.

IV — The Story

A hole discovered,
a promise kept

1974

The warning

Molina & Rowland publish that CFCs — the gases in every fridge and spray can — devour ozone. Industry scoffs.

1985

The hole appears

Farman's team in Antarctica measures springtime ozone down by 40%. The sky has a wound the size of a continent.

1987

The treaty

The Montreal Protocol — the first treaty ever ratified by every UN country — phases out CFCs worldwide.

2016

Signs of healing

NASA's Suomi and Aura satellites confirm: the hole is shrinking for the first time. The ozone shield is growing back.

~2066

Full recovery (projected)

If we keep the promise, the Antarctic ozone hole returns to its 1980 benchmark — a healed sky for a healed humankind.
